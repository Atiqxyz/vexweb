02 THE PHILOSOPHY

LOW-LEVEL AT ITS FOUNDATION.

Broad in
what it can become.

V/

VEX is not intended to be a high-level language wearing a low-level label. The ambition is to make genuine control over the machine coexist with the freedom and functionality needed to build an expansive range of software.

03 THE LONG VIEW

Built for
the long run.

The end goal is a capable language, not a collection of isolated features.

VEX is being developed toward a broad programming foundation: one that can grow from systems-level work into scientific and computational applications, visual software, and substantial projects of its own. The long-term ambition is for VEX to become expressive enough to build a substantial compiler in VEX itself.
