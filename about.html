VEX / ABOUT THE PROJECT

A bigger
idea of low-level.

VEX is an independently developed, general-purpose programming language built around the ambition of combining genuine low-level control with broad programming capabilities.

01 THE PHILOSOPHY

Control
without a
ceiling.

Low-level at its foundation. Broad in what it can do.

VEX is not intended to be a high-level language disguised as a low-level one simply because it compiles to native code. Its identity is rooted in meaningful control over the machine. At the same time, low-level programming should not mean limiting the kinds of software a language can support.

The objective is to make low-level control and extensive functionality coexist. The compiler, language features, and ecosystem are being developed incrementally toward that goal.

02 WHERE VEX IS HEADING

One language.
Many possibilities.

The ambition for VEX extends well beyond its current implementation. The long-term vision is a substantial general-purpose language with the foundations to support systems programming, scientific and numerical work, networking, and visual applications such as 2D and 3D graphics, animation, and scientific visualization.

An especially important goal is self-hosting readiness: making VEX expressive and capable enough to implement a substantial compiler in the language itself.

THIS IS A LONG-TERM DIRECTION, NOT A CLAIM THAT THESE CAPABILITIES ARE ALREADY AVAILABLE.

03 DEVELOPMENT STATUS

An ongoing
process.

VEX is not being presented as a finished language. It is actively being developed, tested, expanded, and improved. Its implementation and capabilities continue to evolve as the project moves forward.

VEX is an immature language which is being constantly worked on and developed by the creator of VEX.
04 HOW IT STARTED

Honestly?
We were bored.

So we decided to do something productive. That something became VEX.

What began as a way to put our time and curiosity into something meaningful grew into the challenge of creating a programming language of our own. We didn't start with a company, a team, or a grand plan. We started with an idea, a shared interest in building things, and the willingness to see how far we could take it.

That small decision became a much bigger undertaking: developing a low-level language with ambitions that reach well beyond its beginnings.

05 MEET THE CREATORS

Two people.
One shared project.

We're both interested in aerospace engineering, building things, and taking on projects that challenge us. VEX is one of those projects: an independent effort that lets us explore language design and systems-level ideas together. We share the work, learn as we go, and keep pushing the project forward.
